## Story inconsistencies and assumptions (unchanged)

- Butcher (voiceover) vs fisherman (visual) is treated as one character (CHAR_002).
- Pier crowd: visual says twenty, voiceover says twenty-five; used 20-25.
- Title says 30 days but the script ends on Day 20.
- The jerrycan is in the shed on Day 19 but the script never shows it retrieved; S22 shows it in the shed and S23 assumes it is already inside.
- Age, gender and base clothing of the survivor are not stated; assumed and flagged in characters.json.
- All in-world text (No Service, NO FUEL, CONTAINMENT ZONE ALPHA, INCOMING CALL) and the Day cards are added in post, not generated.
- The Command voice on the phone is never seen, so it has no character ID.

## B-roll notes

- B-rolls add only details already in the script (objects, locations, weather, bodies of text). Small supporting props such as the pocket watch (S14) and the muddy footprints (S09, S16) are visual details, not new plot events.
- When S34 narration says the ferry was ordered back and the tower was jammed, reuse BR_003 (captain) and BR_004 (relay tower) from S01/S02 in the edit instead of generating new shots.
- Automation: filter asset_manifest by asset_type (MAIN_ILLUSTRATION, INFOGRAPHIC_PLATE, B_ROLL, TEXT_CARD); save to output_filename; load character_reference_files.
